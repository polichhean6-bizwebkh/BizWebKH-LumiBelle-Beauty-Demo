Demo-only limitations

Orders are never transmitted. Admin edits, cart and language choice live only in your browser (localStorage). Sample orders and payments in the Advanced demo are fabricated. The "KHQR" screen is an illustration and cannot be scanned or paid. Stock is not decremented. No analytics, no cookies beyond local browser storage.
